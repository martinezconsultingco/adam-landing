Why ADAM Exists

Chains have systems.
Independent operators have instinct.

The restaurant across the street with 200 locations has a dedicated COO, an operations intelligence platform, compliance systems, and an advisory team. You have experience, hustle, and a gut feeling.

ADAM exists to close that gap — not with enterprise software at enterprise prices, but with a platform built by someone who has been on your side of the pass-through window for nearly four decades.

What chains have

Real-time operational audits. AI-assisted advisory. Compliance tracking. Multi-location dashboards. A COO who reads the data every Monday and tells the team what to fix first.

What independents have

Experience. Instinct. A spreadsheet. And the same operational complexity as a chain — with none of the infrastructure to manage it.

The Platform

An advisor.
Not another app.

ADAM — Advanced Dining Analysis & Management is a multi-mode restaurant intelligence platform. Audit your operation the way a seasoned COO would. Get findings connected directly to what they're costing you. Receive AI-powered advisory that tells you not just what's wrong, but what to fix first.

Built for the operator running one restaurant or five. Not for chains. Not for investors. For you.
